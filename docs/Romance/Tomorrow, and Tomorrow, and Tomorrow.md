# Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow By Gabrielle Zevin

## Overview
- It's a story of two childhood friends who reconnect as adults and navigate the complexities of love, friendship, and creative collaboration.
- Video game development is central part of the plot but novel is about friendship, love, and the creative process, grief and loss, disability and chronic pain.
